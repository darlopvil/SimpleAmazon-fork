This is a frontend for searching Amazon that lets you look up products on the marketplace of your choice, without JavaScript and without being tracked by Amazon itself.

Product pages are served here too, so you can read the gallery, specifications, description, customer reviews and variants without ever loading amazon in your browser. Every image is proxied, so you don't have to ping amazon yourself.
